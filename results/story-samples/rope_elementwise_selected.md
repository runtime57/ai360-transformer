## Котлетка и облако

Seed: 42

**Prompt:**

```text
One morning, Kotletka woke up and found a small cloud in the kitchen. The cloud was crying.
```

**Полный текст:**

One morning, Kotletka woke up and found a small cloud in the kitchen. The cloud was crying. Kotletka asked her mom why the cloud was crying. Her mom said, "The cloud is sad because it has no friends to play with."

Kotletka was sad too. She wanted to make the cloud happy. So, she decided to go outside and play. She saw a big tree and decided to climb it. Kotletka climbed and climbed until she reached the top.

At the top, Kotletka saw a little bird. The bird was sad because it had no friends to play with. Kotletka felt bad for the bird and wanted to help. She asked the bird, "Do you want to be my friend?" The bird said, "Yes, I want to be your friend."

Kotletka and the bird played together all day. They had so much fun. When it was time to go home, Kotletka said goodbye to the bird and climbed down the tree. She went back to her house and told her mom about her new friend. Her mom was happy that Kotletka made a new friend.
